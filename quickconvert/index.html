How it works

One gesture, no dialogs.

01

Drag to the menu bar

Pick up any image, PDF, song or video in Finder and drag it onto the QuickConvert icon.

02

Keep dragging

A small tray opens with only the formats that make sense for that file.

03

Drop on a format

Release and it converts. The new file lands next to the original, which is never touched.

Prefer clicking? Drop files in the tray, adjust quality, size or pages under Options, then press Convert. You can also open files with QuickConvert from Finder.

Formats

Everything you actually convert.

Images
PNG · JPEG · WebP · TIFF · BMP · GIF

To each other, or to a PDF.

PDF
to PNG · JPEG

Every page, or just a range, as images.

Audio
MP3 · M4A · WAV · FLAC · OGG

To each other, with a bitrate choice.

Video
MP4 · MOV · MKV · WebM · AVI

To each other, to a GIF, or extract the audio. Container swaps finish in a moment.

Install

Thirty seconds, once.

  1. Open the DMG and drag QuickConvert into Applications.
  2. Open QuickConvert. The first time, macOS says it can't verify the app. Click Done.
  3. Go to System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down, click Open Anyway next to QuickConvert, and confirm.

QuickConvert is free and isn't notarized by Apple, so macOS asks you to approve it yourself. Approve just this app; there's no need to change any other security setting. Some work-managed Macs don't allow it.

It lives in the menu bar, not the Dock. If you can't see the icon, open the app again from Applications.

Stays on your Mac

Conversions run locally. No uploads, no account.

Nothing else to install

Every conversion engine ships inside the app, including FFmpeg (LGPL 2.1) with LAME, libvorbis, libogg, libvpx and libwebp. Licenses are bundled.

How it works

Drag to the menu bar

Pick up any image, PDF, song or video in Finder and drag it onto the QuickConvert icon.

Keep dragging

A small tray opens with only the formats that make sense for that file.

Drop on a format

Release and it converts. The new file lands next to the original, which is never touched.

Prefer clicking? Drop files in the tray, adjust quality, size or pages under Options, then press Convert. You can also open files with QuickConvert from Finder.

What it converts

ImagesPNG, JPEG, WebP, TIFF, BMP, GIF to each other, or to a PDF
PDFEvery page, or a page range, to PNG or JPEG images
AudioMP3, M4A, WAV, FLAC, OGG to each other
VideoMP4, MOV, MKV, WebM, AVI to each other, to an animated GIF, or extract the audio

Changing video containers copies the streams without re-encoding when possible, so it finishes in a moment.

Installing

  1. Open the downloaded DMG and drag QuickConvert into Applications.
  2. Open QuickConvert. The first time, macOS says it can't verify the app. Click Done.
  3. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down, click Open Anyway next to QuickConvert, and confirm.

You only do this once. QuickConvert is free and isn't notarized by Apple, so macOS asks you to approve it yourself. Approve just this app; there's no need to change any other security setting. Some work-managed Macs don't allow it.

Everything stays on your Mac

Conversions run locally. QuickConvert doesn't upload your files or need an account.

Nothing else to install

All conversion engines are inside the app. It lives in the menu bar; if you can't see the icon, open the app again from Applications.

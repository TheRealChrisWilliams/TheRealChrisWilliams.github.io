About

Senior architect trajectory. Currently embedded in the messy middle where infra meets product.

I work on the part of the stack most people pretend doesn't exist — the polling schedulers, the queue consumers, the OAuth flows that need to hold up under partial failure, and the silent data-loss bugs that don't show up until quarter-end.

My favorite problems are the ones where the system reports success and quietly drops your data. Bulk writes with partial failures. Checkpoints that advance through errors. Pools that exhaust themselves before the first request lands. The bugs that teach you the difference between a wrapper that catches exceptions and one that handles them.

Lately: building Twitter/X integration end-to-end for an enterprise CX platform — OAuth, multi-stream polling, conversation linking, queue handoff to a generation service. Before that: review ingestion, analytics pipelines, and the kind of database-connection-pool postmortem you only get to write once.
